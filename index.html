How it works

Four checks between a prompt and an answer.

ContextGuard sits between users and AI tools. It inspects incoming prompts and documents, applies role-aware policy, then checks the AI response before it reaches the user.

01 / DETECT

Find sensitive data

NER and pattern checks flag names, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, email and card details.

02 / JUDGE

Read the context

Document type, nearby text, requester role and intent shape each decision.

03 / ACT

Apply the policy

Allow, mask, redact, pseudonymize or block. Explain the reason and record the action.

04 / SCAN

Check the response

Scan AI replies for exposed data and hidden exfiltration instructions before display.

Harassment shield

Harassment is about people, not just words.

A polite prompt can still target a real person. ContextGuard checks whether a named person is being sexualised, exposed or profiled, and blocks requests that enable abuse.

01

Consent-aware checks
Recognize requests aimed at real, named people—even when prompts contain no obvious banned words.

02

Block harmful requests
Refuse sexual deepfake, “undress,” doxxing and contact-exposure requests.

03

Keep an audit trail
Log the risk found, policy reason and action taken for review.

PROTOTYPE TARGETS · NOT FIELD RESULTS

For employees and users

Personal data stays protected inside AI workflows. Pseudonymization helps keep harmless work moving.

For organisations

Reduce accidental leaks from HR, finance and legal workflows, with an audit trail for review.

For safer online spaces

Cut access to the photos, numbers and identities that enable AI-driven harassment and doxxing.

Business and scale

A privacy layer that fits the way teams use AI.

Business model

SaaS plans per seat or API call, with a free tier proposed for colleges, NGOs and safety teams.

Integration

API, browser extension or plugin for enterprise AI tools and chat platforms.

India-first

Start with Aadhaar, PAN and UPI policies, then add language and regional policy packs.

Use cases

HR, finance, legal and healthcare, plus campus and social platforms for harassment moderation.
